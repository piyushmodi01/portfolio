<Chapter
  number="01"
  title="The Work"
  intro="These aren't consumer apps. They're tools people use to do their jobs - and they have to work every time."
/>

<Prose>
Internal tools are different. The people using them are not browsing - they're working. They open the tool, do the thing, close it. If the design is getting in their way, they notice. If it's working, they don't.

**That's the only metric that matters.**
</Prose>

<StackedCards
  eyebrow="What I work on"
  c1num="01" c1title="Platform design" c1body="Designing features across large internal platforms. Different screens, different user types - all need to feel like they belong. The job is figuring out what information people actually need and putting it where they'll look."
  c2num="02" c2title="Design systems" c2body="Building and maintaining the shared component library that every product in the portfolio is built on. When this works, teams ship faster and things look consistent. When it doesn't, everyone feels it."
  c3num="03" c3title="Research-led design" c3body="Running usability studies, designing within technical constraints, and working inside legacy codebases. The best solution isn't always the ideal one - it's the one that can actually ship."
/>

<Chapter
  number="02"
  title="The Process"
  intro="I introduced a 5-day design sprint to rapidly scope, design, and ship parts of a product - one week at a time."
/>

<Prose>
Every Monday we look at the product, decide what chunk of work can move this week, and set the goal for the sprint. The rest of the week has a clear rhythm: scope, wireframe, review, hi-fi, ship.

**Tangible work designed and delivered in a week, not a month.**
</Prose>
