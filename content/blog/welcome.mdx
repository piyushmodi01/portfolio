There are a few things I've been meaning to write down for a while. Using code as a design material — not prototyping as a hand-wave, but actually building the thing to find out what's wrong with it. Managing taste at scale inside a design system, where every decision is a policy and you won't be in the room when it gets applied. The seam between AI and design, which is less about tools and more about what happens when generation is cheap and judgment is the bottleneck.

This is the holding page for that writing. Pieces will show up here when they're specific enough to be worth reading — not before. No cadence, no series, no newsletter.
